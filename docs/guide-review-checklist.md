Use one copy of this checklist per exact guide revision. This is an editorial template, not evidence that a review has occurred. Do not enter a reviewer's name, qualification, or date until a real reviewer has supplied and completed the review.

## Procedure and applicability
- [ ] The title and summary describe the actual procedure and do not overstate its reach.
- [ ] Bicycle-type compatibility does not imply component-level compatibility. Specific systems (rim/mechanical disc/hydraulic disc, cable/electronic shift, tubeless/tubular/tube, e-bike retention) are correctly separated.
- [ ] The procedure matches the cited source material; exact bicycle/component manufacturer instructions take precedence.
- [ ] No torque, pressure, wear limit, part compatibility, fluid type, or specification is invented or stated universally without support.
- [ ] Tools, parts, prerequisites, and the expected skill level are realistic for the procedure.
- [ ] Every instruction can be followed in order and any required tools, state changes, and checks are explicit.

## Safety and verification
- [ ] The guide starts with a safe/stable setup and identifies relevant pinch/cut/rotation/electrical risks.
- [ ] Warnings describe concrete failure modes, not just generic caution.
- [ ] Stop conditions are conservative and specify when to stop riding or seek a qualified mechanic.
- [ ] Final checks are relevant to the procedure and do not certify the whole bicycle as safe.
- [ ] Unknown, proprietary, damaged, or model-specific systems are directed to the exact manual or a qualified mechanic.

## Content/source/media integrity
- [ ] Every technical source link resolves and the source is appropriate to the specific claims.
- [ ] Source applicability notes accurately state limits; a source link is not treated as endorsement.
- [ ] Any factual finding and proposed correction is recorded below with a source or technical rationale.
- [ ] Attached media has accurate alt text/captions/transcript as applicable and a complete, lawful provenance/rights record.
- [ ] No third-party illustration, photograph, diagram, video, or article text is rehosted without a documented right to use it.

## Status gate
Keep the guide `draft` until a qualified human review has actually occurred and findings are resolved. `reviewed` is not automatic approval for publication. `published` additionally requires editorial approval and resolved references/media rights. Never fill reviewer fields with the author, an LLM, or a placeholder merely to satisfy validation.
